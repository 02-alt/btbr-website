FAQ

What is Yoin?

A macOS player for your Bandcamp music. It syncs your Bandcamp collection into a record crate you can flip through, downloads albums in lossless for offline, and plays them on a turntable Now Playing screen.

What can it do?

Browse your collection three ways — a Crate cover-flow (Coverflow, Vinyl or Wall), a Grid, or by Artists; download albums in lossless (FLAC, ALAC, WAV or AIFF); play on a turntable with DJ speed control; fetch and edit album credits; keep favourites and import your own local files; and share a private year-in-covers recap.

Do I need a Bandcamp account?

Connect your Bandcamp account and Yoin syncs your collection and can download the lossless files you own. You can also import your own local audio files without an account.

Can I download albums for offline?

Yes. Albums you own on Bandcamp download in lossless — FLAC, ALAC, WAV or AIFF — and play offline. You choose where the files are saved.

What is DJ mode?

Turntable speed control. Slow the track down or speed it up and the pitch bends with the tempo, like a turntable — a speed fader appears on the Now Playing screen.

What is the year-in-covers recap?

A private recap of your most-played albums. The link carries your listening data inside the URL, so nothing is uploaded to a server, and each album links to Bandcamp so friends can support the artists.

Does it need any permissions?

Yoin needs network access to sync and download from Bandcamp, and permission to save downloads to the folder you choose. On first launch, if macOS says it can't verify the developer, right-click the app and choose Open.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.

What's new in this version?

This build adds Radio — endless stations from your own collection, started from a mood, an artist or any album, with picks from Local or Last.fm and saved in the new Radio tab. There are smart playlists that build themselves from your listening (“Rarely played”, “Best of” any month or year), and Liked Songs — heart individual tracks and find them all in one list. Playback, history and syncing are smoother and steadier, too.
