FAQ

What is All in a notch?

A macOS app that lives over your notch as a small pill and expands, on hover, into a Liquid Glass panel that holds everything in one place — media controls, a nearby-places map, a moodboard, Claude chat, AirDrop, a websites shelf and a scratch note.

What can it do?

Control now-playing music from Apple Music or Spotify; see what's nearby on a map (coffee, food, transit and more); keep a moodboard of images, links and notes; chat with Claude; AirDrop and park dropped files on a shelf; quick-launch favourite websites; and jot an autosaving note.

How do I open it?

Hover the pill over your notch and it expands into the glass panel; move the pointer away and it collapses back into the notch.

Do I need a Mac with a notch?

It's made for the notch, but on a display without one it simply shows as a pill at the top of the screen. It auto-positions on whichever display has the notch.

Does it need any permissions?

The media tab controls Music or Spotify through AppleScript, so macOS asks for Automation permission the first time — grant it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac running macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later. If macOS says it can't verify the developer on first launch, right-click the app and choose Open.

What's new in this version?

Everything now lives in one glass panel — the notch expands to media, a nearby-places map, a moodboard, a Claude chat, AirDrop and file drop, a websites shelf and a note, all built on macOS 26's real Liquid Glass.
